## Reading

- Model beats the baseline on Brier in Miami (large) and New York (moderate); it loses in Chicago at every noise level. Chicago is a Chicago problem: Midway lead-1 errors have sd 3.5 to 4.2 F versus 1.9 to 2.3 F in Miami, and the per-month fits there are wider than the ensemble disagreement. Do not trade Chicago until its per-city gap is positive.
- Entry lead is 1, not the spec's 3: Kalshi opens each day's KXHIGH markets at 14:00 UTC the day before. The live job enters at lead 1 (evening run) and adds at lead 0 (morning-of run).
- Settlement cross-check: GHCN TMAX matched Kalshi's settled value on 199 of 204 days (June 25 to Aug 31, 2026); the mismatches are GHCN days not yet published plus one 5 F gap in Miami on 2026-08-29 that needs the CLI report.
- ECMWF runs 1.2 to 3.5 F cold against the settled high at every station; GFS is near zero; ICON 1.3 to 1.8 F cold. The bias correction is doing most of the work.
